## The ownership rule (why the wave can run in parallel)

Each fixer edits only the functions listed for it. When your fix needs a change in a function another fixer owns: write
the failing test, put it in your progress file under "Needs from others" with the exact change you need, and leave that
function alone. The integrator routes it after the merges. Small edits elsewhere (a new keyword argument with a default,
a constant, a test helper) are allowed when nothing else in the wave owns that line; say so in your commit message. Never
reformat or reorder code you do not own.

## F1 ledger and takebacks (wip/fx-ledger)

Review findings (tests: `since-money-tests/`, verifier tests in `since-money-verify-tests/`):

  payer's first remittance on file or not) in a Data-tab meta beside REVERSALS_NOT_ON_FILE_META, and shown on the Data

- money-2: a second payer's share taken back in full is on no item, because the primary's payment counts as covering

  primary's; list it (next step: bill the patient, or dispute the takeback). Do not change `paid_cover` (F2 owns it):
  build the second payer's cover inside `_taken_back_items` or in a helper you own. Real copy: 15 shares, $521.58.
- money-5: the deferred rule skips every later recoupment of a claim after an unrelated balance was carried forward.
  Skip a later entry only up to the negative balance that payer's reversal of that claim left in its remittance, after
  the same remittance's re-payment; never when a re-payment of the same amount offset it.
- money-6: a WO of $500 or more naming a claim loses its own listing when the claim's payments on file are smaller. A WO
  counts as judged only when the ledger applied its whole net amount; otherwise its listing stays, sized at what was not
  applied.
- money-8: fix6's "paid first elsewhere" counts only WO and 72 as a takeback naming the claim. Use the ledger's own
  takeback reasons (WO, FB recovered, J1, CS/72 netting) or its applied takebacks for the claim.
- money-9: a Medicaid denial is marked done once the first payer paid half of Medicaid's rate. Require the first
  payer's payments to reach the item's expected amount before "no money to recover"; below that, size the item at
  expected minus paid, capped at EMA's open balance.
- hosts-4 (moved here from F6: same function as money-1 and money-5): a TAKEBACK item's proof names a different
  reference depending on the order the remittances were read. Break ties deterministically (amount, payment day, then
  reference), and add an ORDER BY to the PLB query. Test on both store kinds and both read orders
  (`since-hosts-tests/test_hosts_takeback_order.py`).
- answers-5, its paid_proof part: for a claim-level never-answered item, `paid_proof` must require every service of
  the claim paid, counting only payments dated after the item's send. (F3a and F3b fix the rest of answers-5.)
