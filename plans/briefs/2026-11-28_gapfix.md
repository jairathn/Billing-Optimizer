## Where this comes from

A private investigation (two engineers, each checked by an independent verifier) traced, on a private copy of the real
store with the 11-27 release's code (ac18813), two questions. Its notes and its failing tests (invented data only) are
private, outside the repository:

- `$S/tk/gap/q1.md`, `$S/tk/gap/q2.md`: the traces (aggregates and finding ids only).
- `$S/tk/gap/tests/test_q1_print_batch_handoff.py`, `$S/tk/gap/tests/test_q2_unseen.py`,
  `$S/tk/gap/r2/tests/test_r2_second_payer_guard.py`: the failing tests. Port them into the repository (invented data
  only; rename them to say what they check).
- `$S/tk/gap/q1/fixed_b/remitwatch/detect.py`: the recommended print-batch fix (diff it against `$S/tk/gap/q1/clean`).

What it found:

1. Of the 131 never-answered or rejected items ($20,113.54) the owner accepted with the 11-27 release, 129 are right to
   be off the list (EMA shows the payer's answer after the send; every service answered for the patient; inside the
   payer's answer time and named on the waiting view; billed at $0). Two VA claims ($260.32) are on no list and in no
   count: they sit in EMA's print batch, the weekly exports' pass hands them to `ema_checks.not_sent`, and not_sent
   waits 14 days from the first charge posting counted to the A/R's own date, so it lists neither yet.
2. 1,557 sent claims ($629,759.19 billed) are counted in meta `ema:unanswered_unseen` and never listed. All are to payer
   ids whose 837 claims get no ERA. 1,542 ($626,251.87) are 2023-2024 visits that no EMA report in the store reaches
   (Production covers services from 2025-01-02, the A/R service years 2025 and 2026). 11 are claims sent to the visit's
   second payer, which the content bridge cannot match because it compares the claim's payer with EMA's primary payer.
   2 are lines EMA's A/R holds open for insurance (bucket "unbilled": held for insurance, no claim created). 1 is a
   charge EMA voided after VA took its payment back. 1 is a patient EMA does not hold.
3. The verifier found more, from reading and measuring:
   - Nothing shows the unseen count. Only detect.py writes `ema:unanswered_unseen`. The Data tab shows
     `insights._feeds_837`'s no-ERA lines, capped at the first 10 payer ids by billed dollars (`[:10]`), each line a
     count without dollars: 13 more payer ids hold 62 unseen claims ($22,005.65) on no line. Owner's rule 3 (blindness in
     dollars) is not met.
   - `_save_ema_wiring` writes a fixed reason, that EMA's claim "cannot be told apart from others with the same date,
     code and charge". It is false for 1,542 of the 1,557.
   - The store archive (both hosts' cycles) retires bills known only from 837s once their newest service is older than
     the horizon and deletes their claims_837 and lines_837 rows. Unseen claims carry no item, so `_held_bills` never
     keeps them: 8 claims ($2,040.23) leave the count now, 299 ($130,436.59) by 2027-03, all 1,542 by 2028-01; at the
     24-month floor a tightened run drops 1,349 ($560,252.45) at once. A silent drop (owner's rule 1).
   - Whether a payer sends ERAs is decided once per payer id over every claim it was ever sent (NO_ERA_MIN_CLAIMS,
     NO_ERA_MAX_SHARE). The owner is advised to enroll VA Community Care for ERAs. About 3 months after that, VACCN
     leaves the no-ERA set, and every old VA claim with no 835 and no EMA claim is listed as NO_ANSWER
     (`_emit_open_claim` has no age limit): about 1,236 items for claims about 97.5% of which were paid.
   - 19 of the 1,557 are second-payer sends (payer_resp S): the count values them at the full charge ($7,702.41); their
     837s say the first payers left $3,511.88. `_emit_open_claim` already caps listed claims at that amount.
   - The saved waiting view names at most 2,000 claims (WAITING_LIST_MAX): 289 of 2,289 are counted, not named.

4. A month-by-month replay of the real data (January to November 2026, with simulated billers acting between runs,
   private: `$S/tk/replay/analysis.json`, `compare.json`) found the same claim under two identities: 43 items changed
   finding id between months (`link|NO_ANSWER` 25, `link|REJECTED_PRE_PAYER` 11, `link|NOT_SENT` 7), and 28 items a
   simulated biller had worked came back as new Work-now work for the same claim ($3,800.41; NO_ANSWER 19,
   REJECTED_PRE_PAYER 8, NOT_SENT 1). The pairs (finding ids, private: `checks.back_as_new.pairs` in analysis.json) include an exports'
   item keyed by claim id and a report checks' item keyed by EMA claim number that are one claim. The gap
   verifier found the cause from the other side: the waiting branch of `run_ema_open_claims` runs before
   `_ema_check_open_claims` reads the newer A/R or Production status, and `run_ema_report_claims` skips only claims the
   exports listed (`_export_claims`, `_export_links`), so it lists a waiting claim's EMA claim with its own clock and
   payer group (15 claims on the waiting view, $3,372.62, are also listed this way).

## What to build (each: failing test first, both store kinds where the code runs on both, break and restore)

G8 (first: the owner's rule that nothing already worked comes back as work). One claim, one item. A claim the weekly
exports' pass or the 837 pass knows (listed, or on the waiting view) and whose EMA claim the report checks would list
must be one item with one stable finding id, whichever pass sees it first and in any order of months; the report checks
skip (or merge into) a claim the other passes hold, including one they hold on the waiting view, and say so in the
waiting entry. When an item's identity must change anyway (claim id to EMA claim number or back), the new item inherits
the old one's person status, notes, faxes and history the way an heir does today (HOTFIX 2d D3: faxes, packets and
history follow an item's heir; a person's decision is never overwritten). Tests: a claim worked under its claim id in
month 1 is not new work under its EMA claim number in month 2, and the reverse; a waiting claim is not listed again by
the report checks; both store kinds. Measure on the replay's pairs (`$S/tk/replay/analysis.json` checks.back_as_new.pairs).

G1. The print-batch hand-off. Start from fixed_b (save each claim the exports' pass hands to not_sent; after not_sent's
items are emitted in `run_ema_report_claims`, deal with each one not_sent did not list). One change to it: never make
work early. If the claim's first charge posting (or its bill date when EMA gives one) is fewer than NOT_SENT_DAYS before
the newest evidence that it is still unsent (the exports' newest day), put it on the waiting view with its due day
(first charge + NOT_SENT_DAYS) and the reason; otherwise list it as fixed_b does. Tests: the investigation's two, plus
"9 days: on the waiting view, named, with its due day" and "still unsent 15 days after the first charge on newer
exports, with no newer A/R: listed".

G2. The unseen count tells the truth in dollars, on every page that shows it.
- A reason per claim: `before_ema_reports` (its service is before the first service date any EMA source in the store
  covers), `second_payer_not_matched` (payer_resp S, or EMA holds the visit with this payer as secondary payer),
  `voided_in_ema` (EMA holds the patient, day and code with a void status), `ema_claim_not_found` (everything else).
  Never the fixed "cannot be told apart" text unless the content bridge really found several candidates
  (`ambiguous`).
- Value a second-payer send at what the first payer left, as `_emit_open_claim` does (later_payers_send).
- The meta keeps totals by reason and by payer group (claims and dollars), `ema_reports_from` (the first service date
  EMA's sources cover), and the store keys of the claims, so a page can list them.
- The Data tab (server and Vercel) shows the total in dollars, by reason and payer group, and names the export that
  would cure the `before_ema_reports` part ("EMA's Production report for services from <first unseen service> to
  <ema_reports_from minus one day>, and the A/R report for service years ..."). It offers the claims as a list or CSV
  through the existing list/CSV pattern of the Data tab, ranked by dollars, behind the names switch.
- The no-ERA lines: no silent cap. Show every payer id, or the first ten and one line "N more payers: X claims, $Y".
  Each line gives its dollars.
- On Vercel (A/R only, no Production) the count is larger; the Data tab says why in one sentence.

G3. The archive never drops an unseen claim silently. Hold the bills of claims counted unseen (as `_held_bills` holds
items' bills), on both hosts, including the tightened-horizon path. Test with an unseen claim older than the horizon on
both store kinds.

G4. The ERA start. Judge whether a claim's payer sends ERAs by the claims sent to that payer id around the claim's own
send day (a window you choose and state, e.g. the 180 days around it, with the same minimum count and share), not over
every claim ever. A claim sent before the payer's ERAs began stays unseen (counted, with its reason), never listed. Port
`test_q2_claims_sent_before_a_payer_starts_sending_eras_are_not_listed_once_it_does` and add the mirror case (a payer
that stops sending ERAs: claims sent after the stop are counted unseen, not listed as never answered).

G5. A line EMA's A/R holds open. A claim no bridge finds, whose line EMA's A/R holds open for insurance past the claim's
due day (same keyed patient, day and code; any bucket, including "held for insurance, no claim created"), is listed as
NO_ANSWER with the A/R row as its evidence (balance, bucket, the A/R's date). Port the investigation's test.

G6. The second-payer bridge, only if it can be done safely in this unit. `claim_answers` and the twin and before-send
bridges also accept an EMA claim whose secondary payer group is the claim's payer, and then only the second payer's own
postings (payment, $0 answer with proof, reason) answer the claim; the first payer's postings never do. A found,
unanswered second-payer claim is valued at what the first payer left. Both the investigation's test and the verifier's
guard test (`test_r2_...`) must pass. If it cannot be made safe, leave it, keep the `second_payer_not_matched` reason, and
say so in the progress file.

G7. The waiting view names every claim it counts, or shows the unnamed remainder in dollars.

Order: G8, G3, G2, G1, G4, G5, G7, then G6 if time allows. Push a WIP commit after each.
