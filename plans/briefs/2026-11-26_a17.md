# Unit a17: the auditor learns plan rates, separate sites, cross-claim ranking and real payer windows

Start when fix7c is merged (the product's rate rules and the auditor's must be judged against the same hand-checks).
Brief from the handoff (section 0, item 4): copy `plans/tools/claim_audit_a16.py` to `plans/tools/claim_audit_a17.py`
(and `plans/tools/a16/` to `plans/tools/a17/` where its scripts change), then add:
- plan-level rate keys (the 835 group number or contract class) and the office-state PA/NP rule;
- the separate surgical site's schedule;
- cross-claim multiple-procedure ranking for a patient's same-day claims;
- a payer window table with its sources (first filing, corrected claim, appeal levels), not a flat 180 days;
- the never-answered twins and $0-to-patient answers (a bare $0 row is no answer: fix7b's rule).
Keep its 182 self-checks and add one per new rule. It must agree with the two hand-checked samples (31 underpayments, 76
dropped never-answered claims: verdicts summarised in plans/next_fixes_2026-11-24.md and HANDOFF section 3); report the
agreement per class. The auditor never imports RemitWatch. Its code goes in the repository (no patient data); its outputs
stay in the private folder, aggregates only in `plans/`.
