## What the pages must keep (the owner's rules, as they show on screen)

1. Blindness is loud. Whenever a feed is stale or the app is in safe mode, every page says in dollars what the app cannot
   check: the header's health pill carries the dollars on every page, and the banner shows where the design puts it. A
   printed page never carries the blind-spot banner (an existing test).
2. Work is ranked by money (dollars times the payer's recovery rate for the pattern) with the basis shown, then the
   deadline. A sort must explain itself on the row (a quiet chance mark), never look broken.
3. The scoreboard (recovered, lost to deadlines, at risk in the next 14 days, given up without a decision, week by week)
   stays, on Results. One definition of each figure on every page that shows it.
4. Every verdict carries its proof one click away. A person can hand-check any item in under two minutes.
5. A person's decision is never overwritten, AI never decides, nothing is closed by EMA's status alone: pages show what
   the rules decided; they never infer a state of their own.
6. Privacy. The names switch governs every patient detail on every page, in copy rows, "Copy all", CSV and titles.
   Browser storage holds only per-viewer conveniences (theme, filters, finding ids, sidebar state, the person's own
   name), never a patient detail, and every read and write of it is wrapped so the page works without it. Page titles
   carry counts, never patient data. Staff names live in the store with an item's history, never in a log line, a
   manifest or a bundle.
7. House style: plain English, short sentences, no em dashes, no code words or setting names on a biller's page (an
   administrator's Setup may name settings), one date format and one money format (DESIGN 2.2).
8. Keep every route, id and `data-` attribute that tests and page code use (DESIGN 4.1 and the design report: `nav
   a.active[data-tab]`, `#view table.wl tr.row` with its data attributes, `#drawer`, `[data-st]`, `#sentNext`, `#xpMain`,
   `#nextItem`, `#prevItem`, `#closeD`, `#xpErr`). Never skip or delete a test. A test that reads old markup moves to the
   new place with the same guarantee (DESIGN 4.1 step 3), and the commit says so.
9. Both hosts. The pages are the same on the server and on Vercel. A field a page newly needs must exist in Vercel's
   dashboard copy (cloud.py's serving tables; bump the serving build the way the code does) and be tested on both.
10. No new network host for the app. Fonts and scripts are served from `/static` or the hosts already in use.

## Checks every unit runs before it reports (CRITIQUE_premium section 9, CRITIQUE_biller "For whoever builds it")

- Screens on the demo at 1366x650, 1536x730, 1400x900 and 390x844, light and dark: no sideways overflow, no script error,
  and no browser dialog (a Playwright dialog handler fails the check).
- At most 8 elements carry the accent and at most 6 font sizes per screen; exactly one filled button per view; contrast
  measured on the rendered text (4.5:1; 3:1 for large text and marks).
- Work: first row at 220 px or less at 1400x900; at least 7 rows at 1366x650; at least 7 cards at 390x844; no row changes
  height when the panel opens.
- A test that the count of inline `style=` attributes in `remitwatch/static/` can only go down.
- The checks live in `plans/tools/ui/` (invented data only, no patient data, no site address) so every unit and reviewer
  runs the same ones.
- The tests of every file touched, then the full suite once in python3 and once in Python 3.11.1/SQLite 3.40 (common
  rules), and both node checks. Screenshots stay in your private folder, never in the repository.
