## What A17 adds to A16
1. Plan-level rates: a plan is read apart by its 835 group number or contract class when its amounts differ from the
   rest of its payer on 3 codes or more; the office state for PA/NP pay comes from the Medicare carrier that answered the
   clinician's claims that day, else EMA's location; a clinician's tier needs 60% of their clean answers in that state.
2. The separate surgical site: CLP08, else the 837's place of service, else EMA's location.
3. Cross-claim ranking: one patient's same-day procedures with one payer and clinician, across claims.
4. A payer window table with sources (first filing, corrected claim, appeal levels): the A17 research's 156 rows, of
   which the adapter keeps the verified, fully stated ones (28 payer groups).
5. Never answered: a bare $0 processing answers nothing; an EMA check row answers only with money, an allowed amount or
   a reason that day; send-day twins one to one and of the same payer; a payer whose 835s stopped answers through EMA.
