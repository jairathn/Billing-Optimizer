## What A17 adds to A16
1. Plan-level rates: a plan is read apart by its 835 group number or contract class when its amounts differ from the
   rest of its payer on 3 codes or more; the office state for PA/NP pay comes from the Medicare carrier that answered the
   clinician's claims that day, else EMA's location; a clinician's tier needs 60% of their clean answers in that state.
2. The separate surgical site: CLP08, else the 837's place of service, else EMA's location.
3. Cross-claim ranking: one patient's same-day procedures with one payer and clinician, across claims.
4. A payer window table with sources (first filing, corrected claim, appeal levels): the A17 research's 156 rows, read
   by the auditor itself (96 verified at a primary source since the review round's UMR row, 60 not); a window it could
   not verify is never called expired. (At this first score an adapter kept only the verified, fully stated rows; it is
   gone.)
5. Never answered: a bare $0 processing answers nothing; an EMA check row answers only with money, an allowed amount or
   a reason that day; send-day twins one to one and of the same payer; a payer whose 835s stopped answers through EMA.

## Product findings: the researched payer windows against remitwatch/codes.py (tip 593017d)
1. Medicare Part B first filing (KY, IN, Railroad): codes.py ends the 12 months on the same calendar day. A last day on a Saturday, Sunday or Federal holiday moves to the next workday, so a claim can be called late 1 to 3 days early. Source: 42 CFR 424.44(a)(1) and (c), eCFR as of 2026-11-24.
2. Medicare Advantage appeals for a non-contracted provider (Humana MA, UHC MA, Anthem MA): codes.py applies the contracted windows (18 months, 12 months, 120 days) to every item. A non-contracted provider gets 60 days from receipt, presumed 5 days after the notice. Sources: 42 CFR 422.582(b), (b)(1); Anthem BCBS Medicare Advantage Provider Guidebook (edition of January 1, 2027), section 7.2, 65 days. This only matters if the practice is not contracted.
3. UnitedHealthcare corrected claims, commercial and MA: codes.py has no rule and keeps the 12-month appeal window from the remittance. The source counts a corrected claim inside the agreement's filing limit from the date of service (its example: 90 days). Source: 2026 UHC Care Provider Administrative Guide, chapter 10, Timely filing of corrected claims (PDF revised 2026-05-13).
4. WellCare Medicare Advantage first filing: codes.py uses the 12-month MA default; the source says 180 days from the date of service. Source: Wellcare Kentucky Medicare Quick Reference Guide, January 2026.
5. Anthem Federal Employee Program first filing: codes.py uses 90 days, because filing_rule takes no plan. The source allows until December 31 of the year after the service. Source: 2026 FEP brochure RI 71-005, section 7.
6. Aetna fully insured plans: codes.py uses 180 days for every Aetna item. The source allows 2 years in Kentucky (participating providers) and Indiana (all providers, from the claim payment date). The 835 does not say whether a plan is fully insured. Source: Aetna, Dispute and appeal process, state exceptions table (read 2026-11-26).
7. Ambetter first filing: codes.py uses the 90-day commercial default; the source gives participating providers 180 days. Source: Ambetter from WellCare of Kentucky Provider and Billing Manual 2026, Timely Filing table.
8. Anthem Medicaid, Indiana plans, first filing: codes.py uses 365 days for the whole group, which mixes Kentucky and Indiana plans. Indiana's plans allow 90 days. Source: Anthem Indiana Medicaid, IHCP Works 2026: Top denials and quick tips, slide 8 (PDF 2026-10-01).
9. Anthem Kentucky Medicaid corrected claim: codes.py has no rule and uses a 90-day appeal. The source allows 365 days from the date of service. Source: Anthem policy G-16001, Corrected Claims (last approval 08/28/2023).
10. Anthem Kentucky Medicaid payment appeal: codes.py uses 90 days, marked verified, from Anthem's 2024 manual. Since April 14, 2026 every Kentucky Medicaid MCO must allow 120 days from confirmed receipt of the notice. Source: KRS 205.534(1)(d)1 (2026 Ky. Acts ch. 179).
11. WellCare of Kentucky Medicaid:
    - First filing: 12 months, or 6 months from the primary payer's payment if later. codes.py uses 365 days with no secondary rule.
    - Corrected claims: within 12 months of service. codes.py has no rule.
    - Claim payment appeals: 24 months from the EOP denial date. codes.py uses 90 days.
    - Source: WellCare of Kentucky 2025-2026 Medicaid Provider Manual (PDF 2025-11-04).
12. Kentucky Medicaid fee-for-service:
    - A crossover claim may be filed within 6 months of Medicare's decision; this is not modelled. Source: 907 KAR 3:005, section 1.
    - Every claim, corrected ones included, must arrive within 12 months of service; codes.py has no corrected-claim rule. Source: 907 KAR 1:673, section 1(1)(b).
    - An ordinary claim denial has no appeal; department decisions get 30 days. codes.py gives a 90-day appeal. Source: 907 KAR 1:671, sections 8(1) and 9.
13. Indiana Medicaid fee-for-service:
    - First filing: 180 days. codes.py uses 365 (also review answers-8).
    - A corrected denied claim: within 180 days of service. An adjustment of a paid claim: within 60 days of the RA. codes.py has no rule.
    - Administrative review: 60 days from the RA. codes.py uses 90.
    - Sources: IHCP Provider Reference Modules, Claim Administrative Review and Appeals (PDF 2026-03-24) and Claim Submission and Processing, section 11.
14. TRICARE claim returned for information: the later of 1 year after service or 90 days from the return. codes.py has no rule. Source: 32 CFR 199.7(d)(1).
15. CHAMPVA:
    - Reconsideration: 1 year from the initial determination. codes.py uses the 180-day unknown-payer default. Source: 38 CFR 17.277(b).
    - codes.py's CHAMPVA filing rule matches '^champva' and misses the remittance name 'Champ Va - Hac'.
16. GEHA (an FEHB plan): first filing until December 31 of the year after the service; reconsideration 6 months from the decision. codes.py applies unknown-payer defaults. Sources: 2026 GEHA brochure RI 71-006, sections 7 and 8; 5 CFR 890.105(b)(1).
17. Oxford Health Plan (a UnitedHealthcare plan): its claims carry filing indicator MB, so codes.py applies Medicare's 125-day redetermination and 12-month first filing, both marked verified. The source gives a 12-month combined window from the original EOB or PRA, and the agreement's filing limit. Any unknown payer whose claims carry MA or MB gets Medicare's windows marked verified. Source: 2026 UHC guide, Oxford commercial supplement, p. 336.
18. Kentucky workers' compensation: bills are due within 45 days of treatment; codes.py uses 12 months. Indiana's rule was not checked. Source: 803 KAR 25:096, section 6.
19. codes.py marks Humana MA's corrected-claim rule as verified, but the research could not find the tip sheet it cites.

The research also found 21 second-level steps (QIC review, external review, OPM review, formal review and others) with sourced windows the product does not model. These are not counted as disagreements.
