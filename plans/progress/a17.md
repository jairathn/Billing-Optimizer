## A17 rule 4, the window table, as now built (claim_audit_a17.py, search "rule 4")
- `PayerWindows` reads the researched form (rows with groups, kinds, length, starting point, presumed mail days,
  source, verified) or the schema form a17-payer-windows/1 (the self-test's invented table).
- Which group a payer is: the auditor's own rules (`WINDOW_GROUP_RULES`: public payer names and ids, CLP06, the
  Federal Employee Program from CLP06 FI or a contract class naming it), read from the payer's own name on the
  remittance the window runs from (`WProd`), never from every name its payer id carries; the payer's own name is tried
  before its id family (a GEHA claim paid under UMR's id is GEHA's). The table's "other" groups match by exact name.
  Checked on the world: every group with a verified row matches the research's group; the rest (3,867 of 437,792 lines
  since 2024: supplement carriers and TPAs outside the name lists, payers under 20 lines) have no verified row anyway.
- How rows are used (`WINDOW_ROW_USE`): the non-contracted MA rows are not used (the practice is contracted with the
  plans it bills in volume, as the product assumes; owner question 1 in the owner notes); Kentucky Medicaid's 30-day
  dispute is for department determinations, so a claim denial's path is a new claim within 12 months of the service;
  Medicare's first filing moves a weekend or Federal-holiday last day to the next workday (42 CFR 424.44(c)); Anthem's
  Kentucky (365 days) and Indiana (90 days) Medicaid plans are one of two (open while both are, unverified between);
  conditions the auditor cannot check (Aetna's fully insured 2 years, WellCare Medicaid's primary-payment extension,
  Kentucky Medicaid crossovers, ERISA's 180-day floor) keep a unit unverified past the base window; TRICARE's returned
  claim is the later of 1 year from service or 90 days from the return; the Kentucky workers' comp rule is unverified
  (Indiana's not checked).
- A window is a `WEnd` (date, verified, latest possible end); a unit's windows combine with `wmax` (open while any is,
  verified only when every one is); `inside_of` gives True, False (past verified windows only) or `WINDOW_UNVERIFIED`
  (past a window the auditor could not verify, or one an unchecked condition may extend). An unverified unit is never
  expired: its item's sub-kind says `window_unverified`, and it is listed apart from the money left on the table
  (`lot_unverified`). Months are calendar months (the month's last day when it has none); presumed receipt days are
  added before counting. With no table (`--windows none`) A16's windows stand exactly as before.
- Self-checks added (31): a second invented store run against the real table (Medicare's 5 days of presumed receipt;
  Humana MA's 18 calendar months ending on the as-of day; Anthem MA's corrected claim from the date of service against
  its reconsideration from the EOP; Medicare's reopening from the remittance; the FEP's 6 months from the contract
  class; an unmatched payer and an unverified row giving 'window unverified', never expired, with guards that a
  verified Medicare window still expires) and direct checks (calendar months, Feb 29, July 4 on a Saturday, the day
  after Thanksgiving, FEHB's December 31 of the next year, group matching, a Cigna supplement plan kept out of Cigna's
  commercial windows, Aetna's conditional extension, a verified row without a length). Each part was broken on purpose and only its checks failed (presumed receipt 3, calendar
  months 3, starting point 2, unverified never expired 4, next workday 1, FEP 2, own name first 2, extension 1,
  unverified rows 1, a supplement plan of a commercial payer 1), then restored exactly (sha256 checked). The non-guard checks fail with A16's windows.
