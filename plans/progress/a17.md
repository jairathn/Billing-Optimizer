## A17 rule 4, the window table, as now built (claim_audit_a17.py, search "rule 4")
- `PayerWindows` reads the researched form or the schema form a17-payer-windows/1 (the self-test's invented table).
- Which group a payer is: first a payer id the auditor's id table names (`WINDOW_GROUP_IDS`: VACCN and TWVACCN to the
  VA's community care networks, 36273 to UnitedHealthcare's AARP Medicare Supplement; review a17-r1-3). An EMA or name
  family with no id of its own is read by the payer id its name is sent and answered under (`Audit.name_pid`: one id
  holding 95% of 3 claims or more). Then the auditor's name rules (`WINDOW_GROUP_RULES`): public payer names and ids,
  CLP06, the Federal Employee Program from CLP06 FI or a contract class. UnitedHealthcare names with a supplement word
  go to the supplement group, and UHC's commercial and MA groups refuse supplement words. The payer's own name on the
  remittance is tried before its id family. The table's "other" groups match by exact name.
- Checked on the world (the windows round, corrected by review a17-r2-6): 153 of 437,792 lines since 2024 are read into
  another group than the research's, 1 of them in a group with a verified row (a self-funded plan TPA). The figure first
  written here (3,867) counted lines the research had never grouped.
- How rows are used (`WINDOW_ROW_USE`): the non-contracted MA rows are not used (the practice is contracted with the
  plans it bills in volume; owner question 1); Kentucky Medicaid's 30-day dispute is for department determinations, so a
  claim denial's path is a new claim within 12 months of the service; Medicare's first filing moves a weekend or
  Federal-holiday last day to the next workday; Anthem's Kentucky (365 days) and Indiana (90 days) Medicaid plans are
  one of two; conditions the auditor cannot check (Aetna's fully insured 2 years, WellCare Medicaid's primary-payment
  extension, Kentucky Medicaid crossovers, ERISA's 180-day floor) keep a unit unverified past the base window; TRICARE's
  returned claim is the later of 1 year from service or 90 days from the return; the Kentucky workers' comp rule is
  unverified. UMR's appeal is the 2026 UHC guide's 12 months from the original EOB (the UMR supplement refers to the
  main guide and states none of its own; review a17-r1-6).
- A window is a `WEnd` (date, verified, latest possible end); `wmax` keeps a unit open while any window is, verified
  only when every one is; `inside_of` gives True, False (past verified windows only) or `WINDOW_UNVERIFIED`. A never
  answered claim's 180 days from the send (the auditor's own allowance, no source) is an unverified window, used only
  when it ends after the payer's own limit (review a17-r1-7).
