## The A17 rules (all in claim_audit_a17.py; search "A17 rule")
1. Plan-level rate keys: `detect_plan_schedules` (an 835 group number or contract class read apart when its own modes
   differ from the rest on 3+ codes and on more codes than they agree; `p.rprod` = product + plan digest, used for every
   rate lookup); the office state (`office_states`: Medicare carrier of the clinician's day, else the EMA location) and
   `learn_state_tiers` / `tier_in_state` (the NP/PA tier a payer pays a clinician at in that state; 100 or 85; a plan read
   apart has its own, `tier_class`).
2. The separate surgical site: `fill_settings` (CLP08, else the 837's place of service, else the EMA location's
   setting) -> `pos_class`.
3. Cross-claim multiple-procedure ranking: `cross_claim_ranking` (`xrank`), used in `truth()`'s paid pieces; a code the
   payer mostly halves is ranked at its unreduced amount (`unreduced`).
4. The payer window table: `PayerWindows` (schema in the constants block, every window needs a source), `appeal_end`,
   `filing_end`, `corrected_end`, `never_answered_end`, `window_end(fam, prod, ...)`; with no table A16's windows.
5. Never answered: `mark_bare` (a bare $0 835 processing answers nothing), EMA check rows classified in
   `load_ema_books` (an answer needs money, an allowed amount or a reason that day), a status alone never answers
   (`ema_answer`), `send_day_twins` / `assign_twins` / `twin_answer` (one to one, same payer), payer-aware unique match,
   a payer whose 835s stopped answers through EMA (feed stopped).
