# Hand-trace of the catch-up of 2026-11-23 (six weeks of remittances, EMA exports, 837s)

Done on private copies of the live Vercel store (image of 2026-11-23 10:19 UTC) with the integrated hotfix's code, before
and after reading the new files through the cycle's own router (`plans/tools/catchup.py`). Three tracers accounted for every
item that closed, left or appeared, and traced samples by hand down to the raw 835 and 837 segments. Counts and dollars
only; the item-level notes stay in the private scratch folder of the session that made them.

## What holds

- Ingest is exact: 1,472 new remittances (raw CLP/SVC/CAS/PLB equal the stored rows; every file one ISA/ST with a matching
  SE; all balance, BPR = CLP04 - PLB, $1,114,937.03), 466 files skipped as byte-identical copies within the batch, 18 EMA
  exports, 1,229 distinct 837 files (37,061 claims) plus 47 copies skipped. No remittance was silently dropped (the
  duplicate-ST02 and newline-envelope drop is real but no real file triggers it).
- Old code and new code give the same result on every one of the 11,075 items they share; the new code also keeps one
  complete remittance the old supersede rule hid (a $655.70 denial).
- Of the 186 items closed as paid, 184 are right (181 paid on the new remittances in full or at the payer's legitimate
  reduced rate). Of the 71 items that left the findings, every one is explained (payments, regrouping kept to the cent,
  resubmissions), except the 12 below whose primary remittance is missing.
- Of the 1,140 new open items, 945 ($190,130) hold up (a real denial, underpayment, bundling, fee or takeback in a new
  remittance, or a claim due by the feed date with no answer anywhere); every deadline matches the payer's rule. None of the
  500 open never-answered items is answered by any remittance.
- The 837s changed no finding (the only rule that reads them, DOWNCODE_VS_BILLED, fired 0 times); all 45 downcode items
  with an 837 match it. People's work: the two items people had worked are untouched.
- Serverless memory: estimated peak about 1,650 MiB now and about 1,800 MiB with all 837s (limit 2,278).

## What is wrong (mostly in the detection logic that runs live today, older than the hotfix)

Prefix T-C: closures and disappearances; T-N: new and open items; T-I: data integrity and the 837s.

- T-C1 (high, 2 items, $65): A technical-component (TC) payment closes the professional-component (26) denial: wrong closure, money off the list. Cause: detect.py resolve_elsewhere (1368): payments matched by (patient, code, date) at 1436 and the item closed at 1507, with no modifier check. paid_events (load, 730), paid_cover (1179) and later_payment (1201) also key on (bill, code, date) only. Fix: Carry the component (26 / TC / global) in every service key: a 26 or TC payment closes only the same component; a global denial needs a global payment or both components; a global payment can close a 26 or TC denial.
- T-C2 (medium, 12 items, $3,494): Unanswered-claim items vanish when only a secondary/crossover answers; the primary's missing remittance is no longer tracked. Cause: detect.py run_ema_open_claims: answered = every claim id with any 835, whatever its CLP02 or payer (2296-2299); a bill is dropped once any later submission is answered (2490). Fix: Count as answered only an answer from the payer EMA billed, or a primary processing (CLP02 1/19/4/22). When the only answers are CLP02 2/3 with OA-23, keep the item as 'secondary paid $x; the primary's remittance is not on file' (sized at the primary's share), so the missing ERA is chased.
- T-C3 (high, 18 items, $2,850): COB (CO-22) claim denials that the same payer later processed as secondary stay listed as 'never paid' (rework of settled claims). Cause: detect.py run_claim_rules (2017): a later secondary processing counts only through paid_cover, which needs >= half the expected amount (1186); settled() (2060) has no 'processed as secondary' case. Fix: In settled(), treat a later CLP02 2/3 processing of the code/date by the denying payer (OA-23 present, any amount) as settling its CARC-22 denial, and list the primary's missing remittance instead (F2).
- T-C4 (high, 317 items, $26,899): Open denials for services that already stood paid before the denial (pre-existing; billers rework paid services). Cause: detect.py run_line_rules zero-paid branch (1868): items are emitted with resolved_by=later_payment (1873), which only looks at later payments. Only the DUP_CARCS branch (1945) checks paid_cover for an earlier payment. Fix: Before emitting a zero-paid denial or bundling item, check paid_cover(bill, code, date) for standing primary payments covering the claim's units on or before the denial; if covered, emit nothing (or born resolved, 'already paid under claim X'). - T-C6 (low, 2 items, $0): Closure note cites a remittance that did not pay the line. Cause: detect.py later_payment returns the earliest of the events it sums (1217 first = min(events), returned at 1224). Fix: Name the remittance(s) whose payments brought the item's own units.

- T-N3 (medium, 27 items, $5,873): Medicaid KY denials as primary stay listed after Medicaid KY processed the same claim as secondary. - T-N4 (medium, 96 items, $2,810): Underpayment items appear from baseline shifts on old remittances and at routine amounts.
